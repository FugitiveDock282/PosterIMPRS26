# News Clippings
Both newspaper clippings are from Ganashakti, a Bengali newspaper circulated in the Indian state of West Bengal. They are both from the same day's issue (24-08-2026), and depict the unapologetic apathy, the neglect and defilement, that the sacred profession of teaching is put through by the governments of India. This is not something short-term, but rather the product of a decade or two of decay.
